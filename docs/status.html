General local laws, universality and general Itô integration are outside the scope of this library.

The Tracy–Widom guide describes analytic Hermite and Fredholm limits, finite Dyson laws, conditional Airy2 probability limits, and TW4 two-tail positivity. The distinct-time producer and global compact-window sign certificate remain explicit inputs.

How to read a result

A declaration and its hypotheses govern the claim. A kernel check, an API entry or a successful documentation build does not remove a hypothesis or establish a stronger statement. The claim inventory and the formalization record are indexes to the source, not substitutes for it.
